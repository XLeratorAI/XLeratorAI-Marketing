THE CLIENT CONVERSATION HAS CHANGED

Capability Is Not Justification.

Your clients face two pressures at once: adopt AI, and justify what it costs. Boards and vendors push AI into every workflow, while finance now sees token bills, license renewals and pilots with no measured return.

The useful question is no longer whether AI can perform an activity. It is whether AI is the best economic and operational choice for that specific activity, or whether a rule, a query or conventional software would do it better and cheaper.

THE DIFFERENCE

Sweet Spot sits upstream of AI cost tools and maturity models.

  • Maturity assessments ask how ready the organization is.
  • AI cost tools ask how to spend less on AI that already runs.
  • Sweet Spot asks whether AI should be doing each activity at all, then proves it in cost per outcome.
Do not optimize an AI cost that should not exist.
USE DETERMINISTIC SOFTWARE WHEN IT IS BETTER

Rules, APIs, calculations and workflow should stay in control.

Fixed arithmetic, state management, enforcement, security, routing and known rules do not become better simply because a model can perform them.

USE AI WHERE INTELLIGENCE IS THE ADVANTAGE

Judgment, ambiguity, synthesis and meaning are different.

AI belongs where it delivers a meaningful advantage over the best deterministic alternative, not merely where it can do the job.

TESTED BEFORE WE OFFERED IT

Built From Evidence, Not a Framework Slide.

We froze the first version of the method and applied it, unchanged, to nine XLeratorAI applications, plus one production application with no AI as a control. Then we revised it based on where it failed.

It separated AI-shaped work from work that code does better in all ten assessments, and produced no strong AI recommendation in the control. The most useful findings came from what a single score could not see: automatic triggers, downstream use of AI output, and whether a human review was a real gate.

PATTERNS WE APPLY
  • AI interprets, code enforces. Models handle language and judgment; code handles arithmetic, security and actions.
  • Facts first, narrative second. Calculate in code, then let AI explain the result.
  • Stage, then promote. AI proposes with evidence; a person approves before it becomes trusted data.
  • Match the gate to the consequence. The more irreversible the action, the stronger the approval step.
